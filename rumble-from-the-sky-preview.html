The Newest Pepper Adventure

A Rumble from the Sky

A cozy bedtime story about a little puppy's first big storm — and the barn full of new friends who help her find her courage.

Pepper is having the best afternoon of her life chasing a butterfly through a sunny meadow — until the sky rumbles, the wind picks up, and the whole world turns dark and loud.

Racing for shelter, Pepper ducks into an old barn and discovers she isn't alone. Together with the animals sheltering inside, Pepper learns that even the biggest storms pass — and that being brave doesn't mean not being scared.

Perfect for children ages 3 to 6, this gentle story about thunder, friendship, and finding warmth is a soothing read for stormy nights and quiet ones alike.
