Pepper the Dog's Holiday Adventure

A Snowy Christmas Miracle

A cozy bedtime story about a little puppy's first snowfall — and the Christmas miracle that brought her home.

Curled up by the fire while her family trims the tree, Pepper spots something magical through the window: snow, falling for the very first time.

Before anyone notices, she slips out into the cold, sparkling night to see it up close. What she finds out there is a story every family will want to read together, year after year.

Perfect for children ages 3 to 6, this warm holiday tale about wonder, mishaps, and finding your way home is a treasured addition to any Christmas Eve tradition.
