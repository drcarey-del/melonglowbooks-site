Cards for the customers the card aisle forgot.

The New Tradition puts greeting cards where they have never been sold: hardware stores, garden centers, auto parts counters and truck stops. Every design is written for the people who shop there, from DIYers and gardeners to mechanics and the drivers who send a card home from the road. A countertop spinner by the register turns every customer into a card customer.

Tools & Garden

65 designs · for hardware stores, home centers, garden centers and tool shops

The card aisle is full of balloons and pastel watercolors. The people who live in their workshops, garages and gardens have been overlooked. These cards speak their language: clamps, socket wrenches, floor jacks, seed packets and a love that grows faster than the weeds. Humor on the front, so they sell from a sealed sleeve; the inside message printed on the back.
