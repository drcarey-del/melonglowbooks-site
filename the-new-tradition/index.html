THE NEW TRADITION

The future is ours.

Card racks are full of the same snowmen, balloons and flowers people have seen for decades. The New Tradition shows how holidays and life moments will be celebrated in the future, with friendly home robots, puppies, holograms, delivery drones and flying gift pods. It’s bright, it’s funny (“Soup by drone. Hugs by puppy.”), and it gets shoppers to stop and pick up a card.
