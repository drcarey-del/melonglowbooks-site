About

Dr. James Whitfield is a reconstructive foot surgeon writing for the people he used to see too late — patients managing diabetes, the caregivers watching over them, and the nurses charting it all in between.

Each book pairs a short, plain-English explanation of what's actually happening in the body with practical daily tools: inspection checklists, symptom logs, and the specific red flags that mean "call your doctor today."
