A Pepper the Dog Mystery

A Nose Full of Wonder

A charming bedtime mystery about a small pup with a very big nose — and an even bigger heart.

When a baby bird goes missing and a coyote is prowling the neighborhood, it's Pepper's nose — not his size — that saves the day.

Inspired by a dusty old copy of Sherlock Holmes, Pepper becomes the block's smallest, most determined detective, following clues that the grown-ups walk right past.

Perfect for children ages 3 to 8, this gentle mystery about courage, curiosity, and using what makes you different is a reminder that no matter how small you are, you can be the hero.
